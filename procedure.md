### Procedure
## Step 1: RBC Isolation
Take chicken blood in 1.5 ml tubes and spin at 2000 rpm for 5 minutes.
## Step 2: Supernatant Removal
Remove the supernatant without disturbing the RBCs.
## Step 3: RBC Washing
Slowly add PBS and spin at 2k rpm for another 5 minutes. Repeat the process at least three more times. Finally, add PBS and store at 4°C.
## Step 4: PBS Addition to 96-well Plate
Take a 96-well V-bottom plate and add 50 µl PBS in each well.
## Step 5: Virus Sample Addition
Add 50 µl of the neat virus in the first column.
## Step 6: Serial Dilutions
Mix thoroughly using a multichannel pipette and transfer 50 µl of volume to the next column. Repeat the mixing and transferring steps until the last column.
## Step 7: RBC Addition and Gentle Mixing
Add 50 µl of 1% RBC solution in each well and mix very gently.
## Step 8: Incubation
Leave the plate at room temperature for 15-20 minutes.
## Step 9: Observation
Observe the plate for the button (negative) and uniform red colour (positive).

> [!NOTE]
> If the sample causes hemagglutination up to a 1:128 dilution, the HA titer will be 128 (2<sup>7</sup>).
